THE RECIPE FOR A LITTLE RIVALRY

Build drinks.
Befriend no one.

You run a boba shop. Assemble drinks, protect your ingredients, and disrupt competing shops. Be the first to reach the agreed drink target.

Official rulebook ↗

01 / Set up shop

Deal five cards each. Use a shared draw pile and discard pile. With 2–4 players, race to five completed drinks; with five players, use four. Agree on three or four for a shorter game.

02 / Take your turn

Draw two cards, or three if you started with at least two completed drinks. Play up to three cards, subject to card bonuses. End with at most seven cards in hand. Reshuffle discards when the draw pile empties.

03 / Make a drink

Combine a tea, flavor, and compatible topping. Ingredients can arrive in any order, but cannot move between your drinks. Use at most seven drink slots. Milk or Oat Milk plus Taro forms a special two-card drink.

04 / Pair the ingredients

  • Milk / Oat Milk: Boba, Pudding, Red Bean.
  • Peach / Passion Fruit / Honey Lemon: Boba, Aloe, Lychee Jelly.
  • Green Tea and Black Tea are universal bases.

Reference & implementation scope

Sources: publisher’s how-to-play page and its current third-edition download (internally marked version 2.1). Reviewed October 12, 2026.

The base box says 2–5 players. The manual also gives a 5–7-player target; expansions support the larger group. This prototype uses the base-game scope, including Oat Milk and Recycle. The gallery presents all 27 base-game card types, representing 110 physical cards. Play with a shared public table and private hands on phones.

Every card effect is documented. Lactose Intolerant and Recycle use user-confirmed printed text; the remaining references are recorded with the cards and in the project rules notes.

Play ingredients directly into a drink. For attacks, choose targets on the table; Sabobatage opens reactions only after all five targets are selected. Everyone may pass or play Customer Loyalty. Undo restores the previous move without a confirmation dialog.
